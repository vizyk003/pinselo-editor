# User guide

This guide covers making designs in Pinselo, from a first page to a print-ready PDF. Keyboard shortcuts show the Mac keys. On Windows and Linux, use Ctrl for ⌘ and Alt for ⌥.

## Creating a design

On the Home page, choose **Create a design**, or pick a tile under **Start a design**: Presentation, Social media, Video, Doc, Website, Print, Photo editor, Custom size or Upload.

- **Custom size:** enter a width and height in px, in, mm or cm. Designs can be up to 20,000 px on each side.
- **From a file:** drop a photo, video, PDF, PowerPoint, Word or Markdown file on the Home page. An image or video starts a design of its size. The document types are imported.

Your work saves as you go. The top bar shows **Saved**, **Saving…** or **Offline**. While you are offline, changes stay on your device and sync when you reconnect.

To rename a design, click its title at the top of the editor.

### Finding your work

The sidebar has **Home**, **Projects**, **Uploads**, **Templates**, **Starred**, **Shared with you**, **Tasks**, **Content Planner** and **Trash**.

**File** in the editor has **Make a copy**, **Star**, **Move to folder**, **Find and replace** (⌘F), **Grid view** and **Move to trash**. You can restore a trashed design for 30 days.

## The editor

- The **top bar** has **File**, undo and redo, the title, comments, **Present**, **Download**, **Schedule**, **Edit with AI** and **Share**.
- The **panel rail** on the left opens **Design**, **Styles**, **AI**, **Elements**, **Library**, **Text**, **Brand**, **Uploads**, **Projects**, **Video**, **Draw** and **Layers**. On small screens, some move into **More**.
- The **toolbar** above the page changes with what you select.
- The **page strip** and status bar sit below the page, with page controls and zoom.

Press **/** for the command palette, or **?** to list every shortcut.

## Templates

- **From Home:** open **Templates**, search or browse by category, preview one and choose **Use this template**.
- **In the editor:** open the **Design** panel, search under **Search templates** and click a template to add it to your design.

Everything in a template can be edited.

To make your own, choose **File > Publish as template**. A snapshot of the design becomes a team template, and later edits to the design don't change it.

To give someone their own copy of a design, use a [template link](#sharing).

## Elements

Open **Elements** and use **Search elements**, or browse:

- **Lines & arrows** and **Connectors**. Drag a connector's end onto a shape to glue it, and it follows when the shape moves.
- **Shapes:** Basic shapes, Boxes and frames, Polygons, Stars and badges, Symbols, Arrows, Banners and callouts, Flowchart.
- **Photo grids:** drop photos into the cells and adjust the gap in the toolbar.
- **Forms:** contact, sign-up, RSVP and feedback forms that collect responses on public links and websites.
- **Tables**, **Charts** and **QR code**.
- **Embed a link:** paste a YouTube, Spotify or Maps link.

**Library** searches Photos, Graphics, Videos, Audio, GIFs, Stickers and Emoji. Some of these depend on what the server administrator has connected. Licensed media is listed under **Download > Credits**, so include those credits when you share.

**Draw** has Brush, Pen, Marker and Highlighter tools, plus a vector pen (P). Press Esc to go back to selecting.

### Arranging

Select an element to get **Position** in the toolbar, with **Arrange**, align and **Distribute** options, **Tidy up** and **Open layers**. The toolbar also has **Transparency**, **Blend mode**, **Link**, **Lock**, **Duplicate** and **Delete**.

**Layers** lists the elements on the page. The top of the list is in front, and you can drag to reorder.

| Action | Shortcut |
|---|---|
| Undo / redo | ⌘Z / ⇧⌘Z |
| Copy / cut / paste | ⌘C / ⌘X / ⌘V |
| Duplicate | ⌘D |
| Group / ungroup | ⌘G / ⇧⌘G |
| Add text / rectangle / circle / line | T / R / C / L |
| Nudge (10 px with ⇧) | Arrow keys |
| Zoom in / out / fit | ⌘+ / ⌘− / ⌘0 |
| Pan | Space + drag |
| New page | ⌘Enter |
| Show rulers and guides | ⇧R |

## Text

Open **Text** and choose **Add a text box**, **Add a heading**, **Add a subheading** or **Add a little bit of body text**. You can also pick from **Font combinations** or search **Fonts**.

Double-click a text box to edit it. Select words to style only those words.

The toolbar sets:

- Font, size and **Text colour**
- Bold, italic, underline, strikethrough and uppercase
- **Alignment**, **Bulleted list** and **Numbered list**
- **Spacing:** letter, line and paragraph spacing, and **Shrink text to fit the box**
- **Effects:** Shadow, Lift, Hollow, Outline, Neon, Echo, Glitch and **Curve text**

**Page numbers**, also in the **Text** panel, adds numbers such as "Page 1 of 5".

## Images

Drag images onto the page, paste them, or use **Uploads**. The panel has **Upload files** and **Upload a folder**, and accepts images, video (MP4, MOV, WebM) and audio (MP3, M4A, WAV).

With an image selected:

- **Crop:** double-click the image, or use the toolbar button.
- **Edit image:** **Filters**, **Duotone** and **Adjust** (Brightness, Contrast, Saturation, Temperature, Blur and more), plus **Auto-enhance**.
- **Flip horizontal** and **Flip vertical**, **Corner rounding** and **Replace**.
- **Alternative text**, to describe the image for people using screen readers.

Drag an image into a frame to fill it. Drag inside the frame to reposition it, or use **Detach image** to pull it out as a separate element.

## Pages

Use **Add page** in the page strip, or ⌘Enter. A page's menu has **Rename page**, **Duplicate page**, **Hide page**, **Lock page**, **Move page left**, **Move page right**, **Copy link to page**, **Download this page**, **Apply background to all pages**, **Resize design** and **Delete page**.

The status bar has **Notes** for speaker notes, **Timeline** for video and audio editing, zoom and **Grid view**.

### Resizing

**File > Resize** changes the design's size. The dialog also makes copies in other sizes: pick one or more sizes, and each becomes a new design in **Projects** while this one stays as it is. Turn on **Scale content to fit the new size** to scale the content with the page.

### Making many versions

**File > Bulk create** makes one page per row of data, for certificates, name badges and product cards. Paste data from a spreadsheet or upload a CSV, connect the columns to elements (**Auto-connect by name** can help) and create the pages. Image columns can hold https:// links or the file names of your uploads.

## Brand kits

Open **Brand** and choose **Create a brand kit**. A kit holds:

- **Colours** and palettes. Click a colour to apply it. **Recolour the design with this palette** applies a palette, and **Save this design's colours as a palette** creates one.
- **Fonts** for Heading, Subheading, Body text, Quote and Caption.
- **Logos**, **Brand photos** and **Brand graphics**. Use **Upload** or drag files in.
- **Brand voice**: notes on how your brand sounds.
- **Brand fonts**: upload your own TTF or OTF files after confirming you have the right to use them.

**Apply kit to design** changes the design's colours and fonts to the kit's. **Make default kit** sets the default.

### Team brand controls

Team owners and admins can open **Brand controls** on the team page:

| Control | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Brand colours only** | Colour pickers offer the team's brand-kit colours only. |
| **Brand fonts only** | Font pickers offer the team's brand-kit fonts only. |
| **Start from a brand template** | Members and designers create team designs from the team's brand templates. |
| **Restrict font uploads** | Only owners and admins can upload brand fonts. |


## Collaboration and comments

Everyone editing the same design sees each other's changes and avatars live.

### Comments

Open comments from the speech-bubble icon in the top bar. Select an element first to comment on it, or comment on the page. Type @name to mention someone.

On a comment you can reply, add a reaction, **Resolve** it or **Reopen** it. Choose **Assign to** and a **Due date** with **Assign task** to turn a comment into a task. Tasks assigned to you appear on the **Tasks** page and under **Assigned to me** in the comments list.

### Version history

**File > Version history** shows saved versions. Versions are saved automatically every 10 minutes while you edit. **Save current version** lets you name one, and **Restore** brings one back. Press ⌘Z afterwards to undo the restore.

### Teams

Choose **Create a team** in the sidebar. Teams share designs, brand kits and folders. Designers can edit, and members can comment.

### Working with an AI assistant

Pinselo has no built-in AI, but you can connect your own assistant, such as Claude, ChatGPT or Codex. Use the **AI** panel, or **Settings > Developers**, to connect it.

**Edit with AI** copies a prompt for the open design. You'll see the assistant's edits live, and a bar above the editor offers **Undo {name}'s changes**. See [mcp-connector.md](mcp-connector.md) for the details.

## Sharing

Choose **Share** in the top bar.

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Add people, groups or email addresses** | Gives them **Can edit**, **Can comment** or **Can view**. The person needs an account. Only the owner can share. |
| **Collaboration link** | **Only people with access**, or **Anyone with the link can view**, **comment** or **edit**. People opening the link need to sign in. |
| **Public view link** | Anyone on the internet with the link can view, with no sign-in. The owner can add a **Custom link**, set **Expires** and add a password of at least 4 characters. |

The bottom of the dialog has quick actions:

- **Download**, **Present** and **Copy link**
- **Template link:** anyone who opens it signs in and gets their own copy to edit. They can't see or change your original.
- **Embed:** code for your website or blog. It needs the public view link to be on.
- **QR code** and **Send to phone**

The dialog also has **Make owner**, to transfer a design, and **Remove everyone's access**.

### Publishing and scheduling

- **File > Publish as website** puts the design online as a website.
- **Schedule** sends the design to connected social accounts. Scheduled posts appear in **Content Planner**.
- A team can publish to Facebook and Instagram through its own Meta app. In **Settings > Connected accounts >
  Team Meta apps**, an owner or admin enters the app's ID and secret, then connects the team's Pages and
  Instagram accounts with Facebook login or a Meta system-user token. Everyone in the team can then schedule to them.

## Presenting

Choose **Present**, or press ⌥⌘P, for full-screen slides. Hidden pages are skipped.

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| →, ↓, Space, Page Down, Enter | Next |
| ←, ↑, Page Up, Backspace | Previous |
| Home / End | First / last page |
| A number, then Enter | Jump to that page |
| A | Autoplay |
| L | Laser pointer |
| B / W | Black / white screen |
| Esc | Exit |

The presenter view button next to **Present** opens a new window with your notes, a timer and the next slide. The audience never sees your speaker notes in **Present**.

## Exporting

Choose **Download** in the top bar, then pick a **File type**. The format that suits your design is marked **Suggested**.

| File type | Good for |
|---|---|
| **PNG** | Sharp images. Supports **Transparent background**. |
| **JPG** | The smallest image file. Has a **Quality** slider. |
| **PDF Standard** | Documents and email, with selectable text. |
| **PDF Print** | Professional printing. See [Print export](#print-export). |
| **MP4 Video** | Animations and transitions, at 720p, 1080p or 4K. |
| **GIF** | A short animated loop. |
| **PowerPoint (PPTX)** | Editable slides. |
| **SVG** | Scalable vector with editable text. |
| **PDF Flattened** | Every page as an image. |
| **ZIP** | Every page as a PNG. |
| **Email (HTML)** | Pasting into your email tool. |
| **Audio (M4A)** | The soundtrack only. |
| **Word (DOCX)**, **Markdown (MD)** | Editable documents and plain text. Offered for Doc designs only. |

Other options, depending on the format:

- **Size ×** scales PNG and JPG files.
- **Select pages** chooses **All pages**, the **Current page** or **Choose pages**.
- **Add a watermark** puts text such as DRAFT on images and PDFs.
- **Credits** lists licensed media you need to credit when you share.

Multiple pages of an image format download as a ZIP.

## Print export

Set up a file for a professional printer under **Download > PDF Print**. Your choices are remembered in this browser.

### Crop marks and bleed

Bleed is extra artwork past the edge of the page, so no white line shows after the print is cut.

Tick **Crop marks and bleed** to add both to the PDF. The label shows the bleed in use, for example "3 mm bleed". It starts ticked when the design matches a print product or is a Print design, and unticked otherwise.

### Bleed options

Open **Advanced** to change the bleed, under **Bleed**. These options need **Crop marks and bleed** to be ticked.

| Option | Bleed |
|---|---|
| **Automatic** | The bleed for the matching print product, otherwise 3 mm. The menu shows the value. |
| **None** | No bleed. Crop marks are still added. |
| **3 mm**, **5 mm** | That amount. |
| **Custom** | Any value from 0 to 10 mm. |

**Advanced** also has **Registration marks** and **Colour bars**, which need crop marks too.

### RGB or CMYK

Choose under **Colour profile**:

- **RGB:** best for digital and home printing.
- **CMYK:** best for professional printing. Ask your printer if you are unsure which they want.

Designs that match a print product and Print designs start on CMYK. Other designs start on RGB. After that, your last choice is remembered.

### PDF standard and other settings

**Flatten PDF** flattens the file. Under **Advanced**:

| Setting | Choices |
|---|---|
| **CMYK profile** or **RGB profile** | PSO Coated v3 (the CMYK default), PSO Uncoated v3, ISO Coated v2 (ECI), sRGB, or your own with **Upload ICC profile…** |
| **Rendering intent** | **Relative colorimetric + BPC** or **Perceptual** |
| **PDF standard** | **Automatic**, **None**, **PDF/X-4** or **PDF/X-1a** |

With **Automatic**, the standard follows your other choices:

| Colour | Flatten PDF | Standard |
|---|---|---|
| CMYK | Off | PDF/X-4: modern print standard; keeps transparency and live text. |
| CMYK | On | PDF/X-1a: everything CMYK and flattened, for older print workflows. |
| RGB | Either | None |

Choosing PDF/X-4 or PDF/X-1a yourself switches the file to CMYK.

### Print check and Extend to bleed

**PDF Print** checks your design as you go. **Print check** lists anything that could go wrong, or says **Print check: no problems found**. It warns about things like:

- Text within 5 mm of the edge, which may be trimmed
- Images that will look blurry in print
- Lines too thin to print
- Fonts that aren't available for printing
- Elements that stop at the page edge
- Colours that will print duller, when the file is CMYK

**Show** selects the element in question. Some problems also have a one-click fix.

**Extend to bleed** fixes an element that stops at the page edge by stretching it past the edge by your bleed amount. You can undo it like any other edit.

- The warning covers elements placed directly on the page, such as shapes and images. It doesn't cover text, lines or groups.
- It appears when an element touches an edge along more than a fifth of that side's length.
- A rotated element gets the warning but no **Extend to bleed** button, so stretch it by hand.
- With **None** selected, or crop marks and bleed unticked, there is no bleed to check.

### Show print bleed

Choose **File > Show print bleed** to draw a dashed line around each page where the bleed ends, so you can place backgrounds past the edge. It only changes your view: other people don't see it, and it doesn't appear in the file.

The line always sits 3 mm outside the page, whatever bleed you chose under **Advanced**. **File** also has **Show margins** and **Show rulers and guides**.
